How it works

Safety

The bike never depends on the phone

  • No phone

    The bike keeps running

    The module keeps the link with the controller on its own. A dead phone, a closed app or an update in progress does not cut the assist.

  • > 0.5 km/h

    No settings while riding

    Above 0.5 km/h, or when the speed is unknown, sensitive settings are locked.

  • 1.5 s

    No frozen values

    With no new data for 1.5 seconds, the screen shows “--” instead of an old number.

  • Verified

    Settings open only after verification

    A setting can be changed only once its encoding has been measured on a real controller. The most important ones need a typed confirmation.

  • 32 km/h

    Street mode by default

    Out of the box, speed is limited to your province's legal maximum: 32 km/h in Quebec.

  • Offroad

    A mode for private land

    It switches on only when stopped, with a 1.5-second press followed by a confirmation. On public roads, a bike set above 32 km/h no longer counts as a power-assisted bicycle. The rider is responsible for how this mode is used.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an Android phone?

Yes, at launch. An iPhone version depends on demand. Tell us your phone in the form.

What happens if my phone dies?

The bike keeps working with the assist level you chose. You lose the display until the phone reconnects. A phone mount with charging is recommended.

Does installation need soldering?

No. Kadans plugs into the original display's connector, with a cable made for your controller. Keep the original display: you can plug it back in at any time.

Why aren't Bosch and Shimano supported?

Their systems are closed, and each already offers its own app that turns the phone into a display.

How much will it cost?

The planned price is CAD 159 plus tax, module and app included. During the Kickstarter campaign, the first 100 modules are CAD 129. The app has no subscription.

Do I need an account?

No. The app doesn't ask for an account, and your ride history stays on your phone.

When will Kadans be available?

The prototype works on a bike with a KT controller. Kadans launches through a Kickstarter campaign, shipping in Canada about four months after it ends, to allow for certification and production. The waitlist will hear first.
